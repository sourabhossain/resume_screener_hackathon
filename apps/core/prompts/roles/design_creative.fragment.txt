@@ ROLE_MATCH_CRITERIA
- Relevant experience: hands-on design/creative work; adjacent creative domains (e.g. graphic design for a UX role) count as partial fit, with portfolio evidence raising the score.
- Relevant education: Design, Fine Arts, HCI, or related creative degrees; bootcamps or self-taught paths backed by a portfolio count as partial fit.
- Relevant certifications: Google UX Design, Interaction Design Foundation, Adobe Certified, and similar.
- Strong achievements: measurable design outcomes such as conversion uplift, usability/task-completion improvement, design-system adoption, or industry awards. Purely qualitative claims ("improved UX") are weak.
- Portfolio relevance: weigh how closely the profile's stated portfolio work, redesigns, launches, or design systems match this job's domain and seniority, and reflect that in experience_match_score and achievement_score (do not emit a separate portfolio score).
