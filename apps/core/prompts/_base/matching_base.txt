COMPONENT SCORES (each an integer 0-100). Score 0 ONLY when the profile contains
nothing of that kind at all. If the candidate HAS experience / achievements /
certifications but they are in a different domain or only loosely related to this
job, do NOT score 0 — give partial credit (1-40) for transferable value. "Not
relevant to this job" means weak fit, not "no evidence".
- experience_match_score: fit of the profile's experience_years and work_history against the level and relevance the job requires. Real experience in another domain still earns partial credit (1-40), not 0.

- certification_match_score: relevance and depth of the profile's certifications for this job. 0 ONLY if none are listed; if some exist but are unrelated, give low partial credit (1-40).
- achievement_score: strength and relevance of the profile's achievements to this job. Strong achievements in another field (e.g. competitive-programming awards) still earn partial credit (1-40), not 0.
